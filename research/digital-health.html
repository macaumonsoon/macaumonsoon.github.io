Theme 01 · Research Paper (Unpublished Working Paper)

AI-Driven Digital Product Innovation Practice for Specific Population Groups in Community Healthcare Settings — A Case Study on Creating Medical Maternal and Child Care Tools

Author: Jiaqun Liu (JULIE) · Independent research at Alibaba Health · Status: Unpublished Working Paper

Preview Full PDF Download PDF

Abstract

This research focuses on innovative design of intelligent digital products in healthcare, specifically examining AI-driven digital product innovation toolsets for maternal and child care groups. Through in-depth user research, innovative design strategies, and practical case analysis, we explore how to meet the diverse needs of maternal and child groups, enhance user experience, and strengthen product competitiveness and impact. The research covers inclusive design, AI intelligent design, and therapeutic emotional design in healthcare. Results indicate that user-centered design principles, intelligent technology applications, scenario-based service models, and emotional design elements are key factors in achieving innovative design for maternal and child care tools. These elements effectively address pain points in family childcare, such as family medication management and intelligent maternal-child dietary management, providing practical evidence for innovative design targeting specific populations in healthcare while supporting the development of maternal and child health services.

Keywords: Community Healthcare; Maternal and Child Health; AI Intelligence; Digital Products; Innovative Design

I. Introduction

(I) Research Background

Against the backdrop of rising global healthcare awareness, smart healthcare, digital medicine, and the integration of internet with healthcare have become mainstream trends. The key challenge lies in addressing various pain points faced by specific populations in family and community healthcare settings, thereby improving the digital healthcare experience and service quality for diverse user groups. In inclusive healthcare, maternal and child groups, elderly groups, and chronic disease management groups receive widespread social attention. With advancing intelligent technology and increasing popularity of internet healthcare, intelligent digital products are becoming widely applied in medical and healthcare fields, providing more convenient, efficient, and professional health services for maternal and child groups. As a special and substantial user group, maternal and child populations have unique and urgent healthcare service needs, opening up broad opportunities for innovative design of intelligent digital products in this field. From the user profile of post-90s new maternal groups, they demonstrate characteristics of intelligence, digitalization, and customization, pursuing precise services, efficient experiences, and refined parenting models.

(II) Research Purpose and Significance

This research focuses on innovative design of intelligent digital products in healthcare, using Alibaba Health's medical community maternal and child care tools as the research subject. Through practical case studies, it explores how to use digital innovation design to meet the diverse needs of maternal and child groups, enhance user experience, and strengthen product competitiveness and impact. This research will positively promote community healthcare scenarios for maternal-child health care and family health management, while opening new perspectives and approaches for design disciplines in healthcare direction, promoting cross-disciplinary development. The research emphasizes exploring intelligent design, emotional healing, and inclusive design for specific populations in healthcare.

II. Project Overview

(I) Project Background

This project is dedicated to developing professional and reliable family healthcare intelligent tools and feeding products for maternal and child users aged 0-3 years. The project aims to build a professional user ecosystem, achieving win-win business and user value through enhanced user engagement and loyalty. The project team conducted detailed research on maternal and child users' needs, pain points, and behavioral characteristics, developing a series of innovative maternal and child tools to meet various needs of this special group in healthcare.

Alibaba Health's medical community digital products provide online consultation, appointment booking, vaccine and health check scheduling, and medicine delivery services, while integrating related resources to provide medical content search and internet healthcare services. Business data shows significant growth in children's vaccination business with continued promotion. Based on vaccine services, offline community mother groups continue to expand, with users through community hospitals accounting for 57% of monthly active users (MAU). The core user group is mothers, with women accounting for 59%, and women aged 19-35 accounting for 45%. These users show high engagement, with average monthly active days reaching 3.4. The maternal and child population (new mothers) shows four significant characteristics: large scale, strong stickiness, predominantly new generation, and high population focus.

(II) Project Objectives

  • Deeply understand maternal and child user groups' needs, pain points, and behavioral characteristics to establish a solid foundation for innovative design.
  • Design innovative, practical, and attractive intelligent maternal and child digital tool products based on user data analysis and market trends, while creating matching quality service experiences.
  • Continuously improve products and enhance user experience through systematic user research, prototype design, testing, and ongoing iteration.
  • Explore innovative models and application scenarios for intelligent digital products in healthcare, providing reference for industry development.

III. Theoretical Foundation

(I) User-Centered Design Theory

In design, User-Centered Design (UCD) emphasizes comprehensive consideration of users' needs, capabilities, and usage contexts throughout the entire design process. This methodology provides a solid theoretical foundation for innovative design of maternal and child tools. Through in-depth research of maternal and child user groups, analyzing their behavioral characteristics, psychological states, and specific needs, designers can accurately grasp users' habits in different scenarios while clearly understanding their pain points.

(II) Intelligent Design Theory

Intelligent design theory studies how to integrate advanced technologies like artificial intelligence and big data with product design to create product functions and service experiences with intelligent characteristics. For maternal and child tool design, intelligent design is mainly reflected in intelligent recommendation systems, AI-supported decision support, and intelligent reminders and predictions (e.g., vaccination times and health check dates).

(III) Emotional Design Theory

Emotional design theory emphasizes that product design should not only meet users' functional needs but also focus on emotional experience. Through design methods to convey emotional value, it strengthens emotional connections between users and products. In maternal and child product design, emotional design is particularly important as maternal and child groups often experience anxiety and fatigue during childcare.

IV. User Research Methods

  • Questionnaire Survey: Design questionnaires for maternal and child user groups to collect basic information, usage habits, and needs and expectations for maternal and child tools.
  • In-depth Interviews and Field Research: Leveraging enterprise partnerships with community hospitals nationwide, select typical maternal and child users for in-depth interviews to understand their parenting experiences, pain points, and needs.
  • User Profile Construction: Based on questionnaire and interview data, construct maternal and child user profiles and user journey maps (e.g., “new mothers,” “working mothers,” “full-time mothers”).
  • Service Journey and Experience Mapping: Analyze users' emotional experiences and pain points at different stages to discover needs and opportunities.

V. Design Strategies & Conclusions

Core design objectives centered on building a trustworthy maternal and child tool (expert endorsement, content trust, brand security), achieving efficient and convenient service experience (intelligent recommendation, scenario-based service design, information visualization), and building emotional and caring design (warm interface, personalized customization, warm interactive experience).

Through user research and practical exploration, this study developed an innovative community healthcare maternal and childcare tool product. The research shows that the key to innovative design of intelligent digital products in healthcare lies in: user-centered design concepts, intelligent technology applications, scenario-based service models, and emotional design elements.
