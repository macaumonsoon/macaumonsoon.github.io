02 / Research Themes

Research investigates the design of Human-Centered AI systems that support cognitive, emotional, and everyday health needs of specific populations. Methods integrate participatory design, reflexive thematic analysis, and iterative prototyping with generative AI, affective haptics, and multisensory interaction.

Theme 01

Digital Health & Specific Populations

Design and evaluation of AI-enhanced community healthcare tools for maternal–child and chronic-disease populations, emphasizing inclusive design, scenario-based service models, and measurable engagement outcomes.

Theme 02

Memory & Cognitive Support

Human-centered design of multisensory AI systems (RetroLight) for reconstructing fragmented memories in Alzheimer’s disease, together with critical analysis of synthetic-memory generation and its ethical implications for dementia care.

Theme 03

Affective Haptics for Autism

Multimodal systems that pair AI-personalised storytelling with synchronised pneumatic haptics to support emotional recognition and regulation in autistic children, grounded in expert participatory evaluation.

Theme 04

AIGC & Cultural Heritage

Human–AI collaborative intersemiotic translation of historical visual heritage (Yuanmingyuan maps) across cultural visual languages, formalised in the four-stage HAI-IT framework.

03 / Selected Work

Selected projects spanning industrial 0–1 incubation and academic design research.

Alibaba Health · Industry Incubation

AI-Driven Digital Product Innovation for Maternal–Child Community Healthcare

15,500 DAU · NPS 47.72

Led the 0–1 incubation of AI-enhanced digital tools and community platforms for maternal–child populations within community healthcare settings. Speculative Service Concept

Aura Healthy — Proactive AI-Driven Health Intelligence System

Concept · Future Health

Future-oriented service system for infant and family health that shifts care from reactive intervention to proactive prediction. Integrates wearables, sensing, digital twins, and holographic support to explore technology-transfer pathways in proactive health intelligence.

Memory Rehabilitation · HCAI

RetroLight: AI for Recalling and Reconstructing Fragmented Memories

Alzheimer’s · Multisensory

Personalised, immersive, AI-powered multisensory memory-activation system for Alzheimer’s disease. Built upon a “memory capsule” database of fragmented patient memories and a “scene cube” device delivering 360° panoramic visuals, spatial audio, and olfactory stimuli. Employs human-centered design and HCAI principles through iterative prototyping and participatory evaluation to advance non-pharmacological cognitive interventions.

Affective Haptics · Autism

EmoLearn: AI-Personalised Storytelling with Synchronised Pneumatic Haptics

ASD · Design Research

Multimodal system that combines generative-AI personalised storytelling with synchronised pneumatic forearm haptics, enabling autistic children to see, hear, and feel target emotions concurrently. Prior to child evaluation, conducted reflexive thematic analysis of interviews with seven multidisciplinary experts (autism intervention, clinical practice, psychology, special education). Distilled five transferable design learnings addressing emotion regulation, personalisation as a safety requirement, touch–emotion mapping, the family ecosystem, and evaluation pathways.

AIGC · Cultural Heritage

Cross-Cultural Visual Heritage Translation of Yuanmingyuan Maps

HAI-IT Framework

Study of intersemiotic translation of Yuanmingyuan historical maps into Islamic geometric and Macau-Portuguese Azulejo visual languages via AIGC. Proposes a transferable Four-Stage Human–AI Collaborative Pathway (HAI-IT): material deconstruction, prompt engineering, iterative generation, and critical curation. Demonstrates the designer’s irreplaceable role as cultural gatekeeper and the tension between structural fidelity and intensity of stylistic transformation.

04 / Publications

Feeling the Story: AI-Personalised Storytelling with Synchronised Pneumatic Haptics for Emotional Learning in Autistic Children

Presents the EmoLearn system and five design learnings (DL1–DL5) derived from reflexive thematic analysis of multidisciplinary expert interviews. Contributes a multimodal affective-haptic design approach for pervasive emotional-learning technology.

Artificial Intelligence for Recalling and Reconstructing Fragmented Memories: Human-Centered Design of Memory Recall Interventions for Alzheimer’s Disease

Introduces RetroLight, a personalised multisensory memory-activation system integrating AIGC with panoramic visuals, spatial audio, and olfactory stimuli. Advances non-pharmacological approaches to cognitive intervention.

Ethical and Critical Analysis of Reconstructing Memories for Individuals with Cognitive Impairment Through AI Technologies

Case study of Google’s Synthetic Memories project. Examines the paradox of subjective versus algorithmic truth, modal and sensory limitations, and implications for stakeholder-inclusive human-centered AI design in dementia care.

Translation of Visual Heritage in a Cross-Cultural Context: A Study on Visual Style Translation of Yuanmingyuan Maps Using AIGC Technologies

Proposes the HAI-IT four-stage framework for human–AI collaborative intersemiotic translation. Reveals the tension between structural fidelity and stylistic transformation intensity, and affirms the designer’s role as cultural gatekeeper.

AI-Driven Digital Product Innovation Practice for Specific Population Groups in Community Healthcare Settings — A Case Study on Creating Medical Maternal and Child Care Tools

Documents market-oriented design of vertical tools and community platforms for maternal–child populations. Provides empirical evidence for inclusive, AI-supported service design in community healthcare.
